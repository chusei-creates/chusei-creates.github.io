How to install

  1. Download the app

    Tap the orange button on your Android phone. If your browser warns that the file might be harmful, tap Download anyway.

  2. Open the file

    Tap the finished download. If your phone asks, let your browser install apps (Settings → Allow from this source), then go back.

  3. Tap Install

    Google Play Protect may say it doesn't know this app, because it isn't on Google Play yet. Tap More details → Install anyway.

Pro is free for testers. In the app, open Settings → Tester build and turn on Unlock Pro to try templates, spending insights, exports, automatic backup and themes.

What to try

  • Go through the short tutorial when you first open it.
  • Make a list for your next real trip. Type items like you'd text them: "2 kilo bigas 56".
  • Tap an item’s unit or section to try the new searchable pickers. Try “kilo” or “pakete.”
  • Paste a list from Messenger or Notes.
  • Set a budget, tick items into your cart, then finish the trip.
  • Set a reminder for your next trip.

Tell me what you find

Something confusing, broken, or an item it read wrong? Send a screenshot through Settings → Help & tips → Message the maker, or on the Facebook Page.

Your lists stay on your phone: no account, no ads. Before you uninstall, export them in Settings → Data so you can bring them back.
